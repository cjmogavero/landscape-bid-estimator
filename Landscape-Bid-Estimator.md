# Landscape Bid Estimator

A local prototype for pricing landscape bids and comparing nursery quotes by total delivered cost.

## My role

I directed the AI-assisted development around decisions an estimator needs to make. I defined which costs to capture and how the worksheets should surface missing information.

## What it does

- Builds a bid estimate from quantities, unit prices, overhead, and a target profit margin.
- Flags incomplete cost lines before calculating the estimate.
- Compares nursery quotes and delivery costs side by side.

Estimators review the inputs against supplier quotes and bid documents before using the result.
